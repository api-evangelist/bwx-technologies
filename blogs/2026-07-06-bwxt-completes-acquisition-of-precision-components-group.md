(LYNCHBURG, Va. – July 6, 2026) – BWX Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: BWXT) announced today that it has successfully completed its previously announced acquisition of Precision Components Group, LLC (PCG), including its subsidiaries Precision Custom Components (PCC) and DC Fabricators (DCF).
